# Feature logos on the Y (game information) screen
The game's features are shown with the logos printed on the back of its case, in the style of its region: **PAL** logo tiles, **US** (NTSC-U) red arrow tags, **Japan** (NTSC-J) white text boxes.
The pictures are generated by `src/openmenu/tools/gen_badges.py` into `src/openmenu/src/feature_badges.h` (cut out of the scans, or drawn when we have none).
Status per region. Send better scans for anything marked POOR QUALITY or PLACEHOLDER and re-run the generator.

## Missing / wanted

- **PAL:** Microphone, Mouse, Maracas, Dreameye, Modem, Broadband Adapter (placeholders); Fishing Controller is cut from a tiny scan (poor quality); a Network Compatible tile exists on the sheet but the menu uses the "Online Multi-play" pill.
- **US:** 2 Players, 1-2 Players, 1-6 Players, Keyboard, Race Controller, Arcade Stick, Light Gun, Mouse, Maracas, Dreameye, Broadband Adapter (placeholders; the 3 players ones can be redrawn from the 1 and 1-4 tags). Online Multi-Player, Microphone, VGA Box and Modem are cut from a 256 px wide back (poor quality). 1 Player, 1-4 Players, Fishing Controller, VMU compatible and Jump Pack compatible are good.
- **Japan:** the wording of the boxes for Online / Network, Arcade Stick, Light Gun, Race Controller, Keyboard, Mouse, Maracas, Dreameye, Modem and Broadband Adapter (shown in English as placeholders). Known wording: 1 PLAYER, メモリーバックアップ N ブロック, ぶるぶるぱっく対応, VGA対応, マイクデバイス対応, つりコントローラ対応, ディスクN枚.

The player pills for "2 players" use 1-2 Players on PAL and Japan (the database stores only the maximum); US 2 uses its own placeholder.
